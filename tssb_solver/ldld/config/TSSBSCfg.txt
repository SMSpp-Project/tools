# The BlockSolverConfig of the TwoStageStochasticBlock: one Solver per
# method of the study, ldld_bench -k choosing the one to run, so that each
# method runs alone in its own process:
#   0  MILP   the deterministic equivalent, integer
#   1  LP     its continuous relaxation, the dynamic constraints (the
#             strong ones of the facility location) being separated
#   2  LD     the dual over the scenarios, each scenario solved as a MILP
#   3  LDLD   the nested form, each scenario solved by an inner dual
#   4  LDrec  the recursive form, the units of all the scenarios at once
#   5  MILP1  the MILP of 0 on one thread, as the duals run
# The nested and the recursive form give the same bound
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
