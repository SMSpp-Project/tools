# The BlockSolverConfig of the Benders form of a TwoStageStochasticBlock

#             certificate gives a wrong cut, which makes the master
#             infeasible)

# method yet: in the Benders form each scenario is wrapped in a Block that
# has Variable of its own, which a LagrangianDualSolver does not accept
