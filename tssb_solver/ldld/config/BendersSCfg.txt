# The BlockSolverConfig of the Benders form of a TwoStageStochasticBlock
# (ldld_bench -b), whose Variable are the here-and-now decisions and whose
# sub-Block are the scenarios: one BendersDecompositionSolver per method,
# with a MILP master, ldld_bench -k choosing the one to run:
#   0  BDS    the scenarios as LPs, the feasibility cuts by the phase one
#             (on a TwoStageStochasticBlock over a MMCFBlock the Farkas
#             certificate gives a wrong cut, which makes the master
#             infeasible)
#   1  BDSLD  the scenarios by the recursive Lagrangian dual, which gives
#             no Farkas certificate and whose own phase one would be a flat
#             replica it cannot decompose: the scenarios must be feasible
#             whatever the master decides, i.e., the instance must have
#             complete recourse (mmcf_tssb_gen -r)
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
