# A txt description of a BlockSolverConfig for the inner UCBlock-style block
# of an InvestmentBlock, to be solved by a :MILPSolver as the LP relaxation
# (intRelaxIntVars 1) of the underlying MIP, without presolve, which can move
# the optimal vertex of a degenerate LP.
#
# HiGHS solves it by the simplex method: its interior point stalls in the
# investment loop, where the same problem is solved again after each
# Modification. On a degenerate LP the simplex of HiGHS may stop at another
# vertex than CPLEX or Gurobi, which changes the path of the bundle and not
# (within its accuracy) its optimum. A ThermalUnitBlock with quadratic costs
# (QuadTerm) makes the relaxation a convex QP, which HiGHS does not solve
# reliably in the loop: there, use CPXMILPSolver or GRBMILPSolver.
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
