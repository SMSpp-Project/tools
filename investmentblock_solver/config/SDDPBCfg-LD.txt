ThermalUnitBlock	*TUBCfg-LD.txt # OneVarConstraints + reserves in Objective
IntermittentUnitBlock	*IUBCfg-LD.txt # zero-max-power replacement

*			*BCfg.txt      # everything else (is_feasible only)
