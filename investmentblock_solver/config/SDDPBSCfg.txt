# The BlockSolverConfig of the SDDPBlock inside an InvestmentBlock: the
# InvestmentFunction takes the first Solver, the SDDPSimulationSolver, which
# trains the cuts with the (Parallel)SDDPSolver and simulates the scenarios
# with copies of the SDDPGreedySolver. The backward pass solves the stages via LagrangianDualSolver,
# whose cuts may lie above the continuous relaxation the forward pass
# computes, the stages having binary variables: from an investment of 1.75 up
# the curves then cross and the training stops [see SDDPBSCfg-LP.txt].
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

BlockSolverConfig     # exact type of the Configuration object

1  # the BlockSolverConfig is a "differential" one

3  # number of (the names of) Solver in this BlockSolverConfig
# now all the names of the Solver - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
SDDPSimulationSolver  # name of 1st Solver (training and simulation)
SDDPGreedySolver      # name of 2nd Solver (the simulator)
ParallelSDDPSolver    # name of 3rd Solver (the trainer)

3  # number of ComputeConfig in this BlockSolverConfig

# now all the ComputeConfig - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
*                     # the SDDPSimulationSolver has no parameter
*GreedyCfg.txt
*SDDPCfg.txt
